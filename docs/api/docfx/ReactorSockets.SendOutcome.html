The outcome of one Send(PeerId, ReadOnlySpan<byte>) call. A bare enum rather than a result struct (unlike SendResult): a Session-level send has no partial-completion quantity worth reporting back — the framed Message either fit in the Peer's retention region whole, or it did not, so the outcome is the whole story.

public enum SendOutcome

Fields

Sent = 0

The framed Message was copied whole into the Peer's retention region.

RetentionExhausted = 1

The Peer's retention region had no room for the framed Message. Nothing was copied and nothing about the Session changed — the caller may retry once bytes have been released, or otherwise.

Closed = 2

A Goodbye for this Peer has already passed in one direction or the other — this end sent its own (Close), or the far end's arrived and the Peer is Closing. Either way the Session is ending and nothing was copied: a Message queued after a Goodbye would have to either jump it in retention or follow it, and neither is a Message the far end has any business receiving.

MessageTooLarge = 3

The Message is wider than one Datagram can carry, and the Space it was sent on cannot fragment it. Nothing was copied. Deliberately its own value rather than folded into RetentionExhausted: retention exhaustion is backpressure the caller can retry once bytes are released, while an oversized Message will never fit however long the caller waits — a consumer that cannot tell the two apart cannot tell "retry later" from "split it or use a reliable mode".

RemoteMessageTooLarge = 4

A Message, in any delivery mode, exceeds the MaxMessageSize the remote end announced during the handshake. Nothing was copied, and the caller may retry immediately with a smaller payload. An unreliable Message over both limits reports MessageTooLarge instead. Distinct from MessageTooLarge purely by whose limit was exceeded: that value means a local configuration fact the caller's own settings could have predicted (the single-Datagram cap an unreliable mode cannot exceed); this one means the far end's announced receive limit, which the caller has no way to know in advance.

NotEstablished = 5

The Peer's handshake has not completed: a dial is still connecting, or waiting for the far end's handshake record. Nothing was copied and nothing about the Session changed. Wait for PeerConnected for this Peer before Sending. Refusing here rather than queueing keeps a Message from reaching a socket whose connect has not resolved, or going out before this end knows the far end's version and announced MaxMessageSize.

Remarks

Deliberately its own type rather than folded into DisconnectReason: exhausting retention does not end the Session, so it belongs on the return of the call that can produce it rather than mixed into the roster of reasons a Peer is gone.

Explicit values throughout, the same house style as DisconnectReason: a member is added at the end with the next free number, never inserted, so a value a consumer has already persisted keeps its meaning across a library upgrade.
