Which kind of RawEvent a drained event is (ADR 0008). Accepted comes from a listening Link, Connected from dialling, SegmentReceived and EndOfStream from stream reads, and Writable from partial sends.

public enum RawEventKind

Fields

DatagramReceived = 0

A datagram arrived on a bound UDP Link. Link, Source and Payload are populated.

Closed = 1

A Link closed. Link and Reason are populated.

Accepted = 2

A connection was accepted on a listening Link, inside the Pump that found it ready. ListeningLink names the listening Link that fired; Link names the new connection, already registered and consuming a capacity slot of its own.

Connected = 3

An outbound dial completed successfully, inside the Pump that found the connecting socket writable. Link is populated; a dial that fails instead produces Closed with Refused or Unreachable.

SegmentReceived = 4

A run of bytes arrived on a stream Link. Link and Payload are populated. Carries no claim of being a whole anything — a counterparty's single write may be drained as one or several of these, and framing is the consumer's decision, not this library's.

EndOfStream = 5

The remote end of a stream Link will send nothing further while this Link remains open for sending — TCP's own half-close, kept separate from Closed because collapsing them would decide on the consumer's behalf that their protocol cannot use half-close. Link is populated. A consumer that never calls Close after this holds the Link and its slot until the Reactor is disposed — there is no Linger in Raw to reclaim it.

Writable = 6

A stream Link that took fewer bytes than offered on a previous Send has become writable again. Link is populated. Armed, not level-triggered: exactly one of these follows a short send, and a Link never over-sent on produces none at all — a reply to something the consumer did, not a status the Reactor volunteers every Pump a socket happens to be writable (which is nearly always).
