Close(PeerId)

Ends the Session with peer and queues a PeerClosed carrying LocalClose. Idempotent: a stale PeerId is a no-op, because a Peer closing under a caller is an ordinary race rather than a programmer error.

public void Close(PeerId peer)

Parameters

peer PeerId

Remarks

This slice closes locally and says nothing to the remote. The Goodbye exchange, and the Linger that lets it be retransmitted, belong to the slice that implements them; until then a closed Peer is discovered on the far side by its silence.
