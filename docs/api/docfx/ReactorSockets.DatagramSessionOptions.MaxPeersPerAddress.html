MaxPeersPerAddress

The most accepted Peers one address may hold at once, so one reachable address cannot occupy every capacity Slot. Defaults to ResolvedMaxPeersPerAddress, one fewer than Capacity. Provenance: derived from capacity, as the largest limit that still keeps one address from holding every Slot (ADR 0014). How many Peers legitimately share an address depends on the NATs and proxies in front of this Reactor, so set it lower when strangers can reach it. At least 1. A value of Capacity or above sets no limit, which is what the default is at a capacity of 1.

Remarks

The count is by address, without the port: a host can answer a Cookie from as many ports as it can bind, so a limit that counted ports would limit nothing. The address family and IPv6 scope stay in the key, because they distinguish hosts (ADR 0014). IPv6 addresses are counted by the prefix PeerLimitIPv6PrefixLength sets, so a host that can bind a whole prefix gets the limit once. IPv4 addresses are counted whole.

A Peer is charged to the address its handshake came from for as long as it holds its Slot, including while Interrupted or Closing and after a Resume moves it to another address. A dialled Peer is not charged: this Reactor chose to make it. A handshake over the limit is refused the same way as one at a full Reactor, and reported with AddressPeerLimitReached.
