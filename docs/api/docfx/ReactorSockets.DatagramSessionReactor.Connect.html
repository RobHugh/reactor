Connect(IPEndPoint)

Begins a Session with destination, returning the PeerId that will address it. The Peer occupies a capacity Slot from this moment, exactly as a dialling Link does in RawReactor: the handshake is work this Reactor is holding, and pretending otherwise would let a consumer dial past capacity and discover it only on the far side of a round trip. PeerConnected or PeerClosed resolves it on a later Pump.

Remarks

While the dial is pending, a Pump resends its current flight: the Hello while it awaits a Cookie, and the Hello-with-cookie while it awaits an Accept. The delay starts at the observed Pump interval, doubles, stops at this Reactor's Linger and is drawn with full jitter, the same schedule the stream profile re-dials on. A dial whose cookie is one epoch old restarts from a fresh Hello. A dial still pending one Linger after this call ends with TransportFailed and frees its Slot. Close(PeerId) ends it sooner and stops the resends. Nothing is resent while the clock has not moved since the last send.
