Remarks

The shape is shared by every profile; which members move is not. DatagramsSent and DatagramsReceived stay at zero on a Reactor carrying no datagram transport, and the two admission counters divide the same way. A member that never moves on a profile is reporting a true zero, not a gap.

Properties

AdmissionDeferrals

Admissions declined that left the work where it was, for a later Pump to take. Moves on the transports where declined work waits — Raw and the stream Session profile. Counted once per declining rather than once per piece of work turned away, which makes it coarse where the work sits in an OS backlog: one listening Link or bound endpoint declines its whole backlog with one declining, because that depth is unknowable without accepting. Where the Reactor already holds the work — a stream Session handshake accepted but not completed — one declining is one piece of work and the figure is exact. Read the magnitude as "how much of the listening fleet is turning work away", never as backlog depth.

AdmissionDiscards

Admissions declined that let the work go, because the transport gave it nowhere to wait. Moves only on the datagram Session profile, which Discards the Hellos it cannot admit. Always an exact count of work turned away, which AdmissionDeferrals is not, because a Reactor can only Discard what it had in hand. Kept apart from Deferrals because the two differ in the one way a consumer would act on: deferred work arrives on a later Pump, Discarded work never does.

UnknownSourceDiscards

Datagrams taken from the transport and thrown away because nothing could be attributed to a Peer: a malformed control datagram, or a session id naming no Session. Reactor-scope rather than per-Peer precisely because there is no Peer to charge them to — the whole point of the member is the traffic that failed to name one. Moves only on the datagram Session profile. A rising figure on a Reactor that is otherwise healthy is a scan or a flood, not a fault, which is why the matching Diagnostics are Warn.
