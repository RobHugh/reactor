Which kind of SessionEvent was drained, and therefore which of its fields carry meaning. Shared by both Session profiles (ADR 0010).

public enum SessionEventKind

Fields

PeerConnected = 1

A Session is established and the Peer is addressable. Raised on both ends: on the dialler when the Accept arrives, on the accepting end when a Hello is admitted.

PeerClosed = 2

A Session ended, or a dial never became one. Reason says why, and the PeerId is dead from this event onwards.

MessageReceived = 3

A whole Message was reassembled from the Peer's stream and copied into the frame arena. Payload carries it, valid only until the next Pump (ADR 0008).

PeerClosing = 4

The Peer's Goodbye arrived while something was still outstanding to deliver: either Messages this end had already reassembled from it but the consumer had not yet drained (even though it reached MessageReceived first), or bytes still buffered in its receive share awaiting arena room, or this end's own outbound retention for it still being flushed to the socket. The Peer accepts no further Sends worth making from here and a PeerClosed carrying PeerGoodbye follows once all of that is exhausted. Never raised when nothing was outstanding on either side — a Goodbye that finds the Peer already caught up closes it directly, with no zero-Frame Closing observable in between. In the datagram profile the Goodbye acts at its turn in ReliableOrdered send order, and "outstanding" means Messages received but not yet delivered.

PeerInterrupted = 5

The stream profile's transport for this Peer is gone, but the Peer itself is not: its PeerId — including generation — stays live, CountersOf(PeerId) keeps answering for it, and its undelivered inbound Messages and outbound retention ring are held rather than discarded (ADR 0010). Raised whenever an established Peer's transport ends without a Goodbye first: an inbound FIN carrying nothing further, a reset, a local write failure, or this end's own receive-idle deadline expiring (which closed the Peer outright before this kind existed). Distinct from PeerClosing: Closing means a Goodbye arrived and the Session is ending on purpose, with PeerClosed to follow once both sides drain — nothing here is ever resumable. Interrupted means the opposite — no Goodbye was ever seen, the Session is not ending, and the Peer sits ready for a Resume for as long as Linger allows. A Peer that sent Goodbye and then a bare FIN reaches PeerClosing or PeerClosed, never this. Carries only Peer — there is no DisconnectReason to report, because nothing has ended.

PeerResumed = 6

A Session held PeerInterrupted reclaimed its transport and is delivering again. Raised on whichever side completes the match, and in both cases only once that side knows where to resend from: on the listening side from ResumeHeldPeer, once the peer's Resume record checks out and its carried delivery counter has driven the rewind; on the dialling side not from ResumeDialledPeer, which only sends the Resume record, but later, when the listening side's reciprocal delivery-counter record arrives and is rewound off. Only that success path reaches here: a resume refused because the peer named a Message this end no longer retains closes the Peer instead (RetentionGap) and never raises this. Peer's PeerId, generation included, is exactly the one PeerInterrupted carried — a Resume is the same Peer's transport coming back, never a new one — and no PeerClosed is ever observed between the two. The consumer is asked for no reconciliation: Messages sent before the interruption arrive without being re-sent, since the rewind that makes this event possible already put this end's outbound retention back to the peer's last confirmed point before this event is raised. Carries only Peer, the same as PeerInterrupted — nothing has ended, so there is no DisconnectReason to report.
