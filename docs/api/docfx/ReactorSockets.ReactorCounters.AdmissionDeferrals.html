AdmissionDeferrals

Admissions declined that left the work where it was, for a later Pump to take. Moves on the transports where declined work waits — Raw and the stream Session profile. On the stream profile both the admission cap and the limit of Capacity connections held waiting for their first record decline this way. Counted once per declining rather than once per piece of work turned away, which makes it coarse where the work sits in an OS backlog: one listening Link or bound endpoint declines its whole backlog with one declining, because that depth is unknowable without accepting. Where the Reactor already holds the work — a stream Session handshake accepted but not completed — one declining is one piece of work and the figure is exact. Read the magnitude as "how much of the listening fleet is turning work away", never as backlog depth. Not derivable: no other counter sees work the Reactor never accepted, and its unit is declinings, not the pieces of work AdmissionDiscards counts.
