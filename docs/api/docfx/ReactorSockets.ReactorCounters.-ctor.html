ReactorCounters(ulong, ulong, ulong, ulong, ulong, ulong, ulong, ulong)

A point-in-time snapshot of the Reactor-scope counters (ADR 0013): monotonic figures running from construction, with no reset. Fetched by value so nothing lets a consumer hold a pointer into live state across a Pump; diffing two snapshots gives everything a reset would, from state the consumer already owns.

public ReactorCounters(ulong SinkFaulted, ulong DatagramsSent, ulong DatagramsReceived, ulong ArenaHighWaterBytes, ulong AdmissionDeferrals, ulong AdmissionDiscards, ulong UnknownSourceDatagrams, ulong MalformedSessionDatagrams)

Parameters

SinkFaulted ulong

Times the installed Sink threw and was disabled for the Reactor's life. Not derivable: the Sink is the only other place this could be reported, and it is the thing that failed.

DatagramsSent ulong

Datagrams handed to the transport for sending. Not derivable: per-Peer byte figures cover only traffic a Peer exists to charge and end with the Peer, while this counts every datagram for the Reactor's life.

DatagramsReceived ulong

Datagrams taken from the transport. Not derivable: it is the denominator for UnknownSourceDatagrams and MalformedSessionDatagrams, and per-Peer figures lose closed Peers and never see unattributed traffic.

ArenaHighWaterBytes ulong

The most Arena bytes any single Frame has used. Moves on every profile. Not derivable: byte totals say how much arrived over time, never how much of it landed in one Frame, which is the figure that sizes the Arena.

AdmissionDeferrals ulong

Admissions declined that left the work where it was, for a later Pump to take. Moves on the transports where declined work waits — Raw and the stream Session profile. Counted once per declining rather than once per piece of work turned away, which makes it coarse where the work sits in an OS backlog: one listening Link or bound endpoint declines its whole backlog with one declining, because that depth is unknowable without accepting. Where the Reactor already holds the work — a stream Session handshake accepted but not completed — one declining is one piece of work and the figure is exact. Read the magnitude as "how much of the listening fleet is turning work away", never as backlog depth. Not derivable: no other counter sees work the Reactor never accepted, and its unit is declinings, not the pieces of work AdmissionDiscards counts.

AdmissionDiscards ulong

Admissions declined that let the work go, because the transport gave it nowhere to wait. Moves only on the datagram Session profile, which Discards the Hellos it cannot admit, whether the admission cap, capacity or MaxPeersPerAddress declined them. The Diagnostics tell the three apart. Always an exact count of work turned away, which AdmissionDeferrals is not, because a Reactor can only Discard what it had in hand. Kept apart from Deferrals because the two differ in the one way a consumer would act on: deferred work arrives on a later Pump, Discarded work never does. Not derivable: the two admission counters are never both non-zero on one Reactor, yet knowing one says nothing of the other, and their units differ, so one shared field would invite a sum or comparison that means nothing.

UnknownSourceDatagrams ulong

Datagrams taken from the transport and thrown away because nothing could be attributed to a Peer: a malformed control datagram, or a session id naming no Session. Not a Discard in the AdmissionDiscards sense: no Admission was declined and no cap was spent, the bytes named nobody. A data datagram whose session id did name a live Session and then failed to parse is MalformedSessionDatagrams instead. Reactor-scope rather than per-Peer precisely because there is no Peer to charge them to — the whole point of the member is the traffic that failed to name one. Moves only on the datagram Session profile. A rising figure on a Reactor that is otherwise healthy is a scan or a flood, not a fault, which is why the matching Diagnostics are Warn. Not derivable: no Peer exists to carry it, and DatagramsReceived counts these together with everything else.

MalformedSessionDatagrams ulong

Data datagrams thrown away for failing to parse after their session id named a live Session: a short or bad header, a record that does not read, an illegal Fragment set. Kept apart from UnknownSourceDatagrams because the two say different things about who is sending — this traffic did name a Peer, so it is either an established Peer sending bytes this Reactor cannot read or a forgery that guessed a live id, where the other figure is traffic that named nobody at all. Reactor-scope like its neighbour rather than per-Peer: a datagram whose body did not parse is not proven to have come from the Peer whose id it carries, and charging it there would let a stranger move an established Peer's figures. Moves only on the datagram Session profile, and its Diagnostic is the same DatagramMalformed. Not derivable: the Peer it names never counts it, and UnknownSourceDatagrams counts only traffic that named nobody.

Remarks

The shape is shared by every profile; which members move is not. DatagramsSent and DatagramsReceived stay at zero on a Reactor carrying no datagram transport, and the two admission counters divide the same way. A member that never moves on a profile is reporting a true zero, not a gap. Every member says, under "Not derivable", what it tells a consumer that the other counters do not (ADR 0013). A field that could not be given that line would not exist.
